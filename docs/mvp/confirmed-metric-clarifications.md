These user decisions supersede earlier roster and shared-definition proposals.

- Advocacy and Operations are cross-department tracking areas, not departments. Measures and action items can be assigned to any actual department. Community Relations can contribute advocacy-related work without changing its departmental ownership.
- Keep resident satisfaction, survey response and controllable-cost measures independent by department. No forced deduplication or population reconciliation. Property Management records its controllable-cost assessment; Manager, Enterprise Initiatives and COO verification may remain outside the app.
- Use the latest strategy wording. Keep stable internal IDs but hide number codes. Keep the two questioned Business Model Elements objectives in their supplied placement.
- The empty Q3 pipeline slot is not a separate objective: use Advance College Ave Phase 2 Closing once. Digitize the Leasing Experience is confirmed.
- Revenue diversification has three streams composed of management fees; portfolio distributions/residual receipts/deferred fee payments; and contributed revenue. These are descriptions, not approved bucket titles.
- Confirmed bindings: PM fee plus Asset Management Fees; Finance's Excess Cash to Parent plus Deferred Developer Fee Payments; Community Relations' contributed revenue total. Asset Management Fees is now an explicit tracked metric. Distributions and residual receipts are out of scope.
- Excess Cash to Parent means amounts properties owe back to HDC. It remains a separately visible Finance metric linked to the 2026 Financial Resiliency workplan while contributing to the portfolio stream.
- CR contribution entries specify category, amount, reporting period and description. Categories include grant, NeighborWorks, individual gift, corporate contribution and other. One contribution receives one category. Contributed revenue uses the total OR its nonoverlapping components, never both. This input contract is mapped, not yet a persisted entry form.
- Calculate each stream's share against the total across all three, for the same period and currency. Keep 33/33/33 as the supplied target display; color tolerance remains unspecified. Do not classify incomplete source coverage as a complete mix.
